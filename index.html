Method and limits

  • Every claim carries four pieces of evidence: the price, the median it is compared with, how many data points that median rests on, and the date we observed it.
  • Our sources are cached quotes: the Aviasales fare cache for flights, and the TripAdvisor OTA panel (read through Xotelo) for hotels. They are not live searches. The live price can differ.
  • A price in our records does not mean a room or seat can be booked. When a check on the OTA's own page finds a date sold out everywhere we looked, we stop showing that date until a newer check says otherwise.
  • Prices are references in USD: flights one-way; hotels per night for a 2-night stay starting on a Friday. Taxes and fees may or may not be included, depending on the OTA.
  • Two kinds of claims. 'Down vs its 30-day median' compares one hotel date or flight with its own history and needs at least 7 earlier data points. 'Cheapest of the Fridays we track' compares dates inside one snapshot and is not a price drop.
  • A rate change on our hotel panel can come from the hotel or from a different OTA becoming the cheapest. We say which OTA was cheapest; we do not claim the hotel cut its price.
  • We do not write anything we cannot back with our own records. Corrections are logged in the same records shown above.

Setiap klaim membawa empat bukti: harga, median pembanding, jumlah titik data, dan tanggal pengamatan. Sumber kami kutipan cache (Aviasales, panel OTA TripAdvisor lewat Xotelo), bukan pencarian langsung. Harga di arsip tidak menjamin kamar atau kursi bisa dipesan. Harga acuan dalam USD: penerbangan sekali jalan, hotel per malam untuk 2 malam mulai Jumat. Dua jenis klaim: turun dibanding median 30 harinya sendiri, dan Jumat termurah dalam satu pengamatan (bukan penurunan harga). Koreksi dicatat di log yang sama.
